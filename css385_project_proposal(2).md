Elevator pitch  
Do you enjoy fast-paced 2D platformers with shooting, climbing, and head-to-head competition? Ascending Arena is the ultimate challenge for players who love 2D platforming, competitive shooting, and high-stakes vertical races. Compete against other players to claim victory\!

Synopsis  
In this game, you face off against opponents and try to outpace each other by skillfully outclimbing and out-aiming your rivals while the floor constantly disappears beneath you from rising lava. Success relies on you quickly seizing the powerful weapons and abilities dropped onto the map, which give you the edge needed to survive the ascent of these moving platforms.This exciting combination of strategic platforming and explosive utilities creates a perfect balance of predictable skill and chaotic outcomes in every single match. 

Objective  
Challenge players by having them compete against one another using a variety of weapons and strategies.

Mechanics  
Players will use WASD to move their character and jump onto higher platforms, and they will also be able to pick up weapons by pressing E. They will be able to use their weapon by aiming with their mouse and left-clicking to fire their weapon at their opponent to win. After a gun runs out of bullets, the player can throw the weapon as a projectile at the enemy for an extra hit.

Players will start on the same platform level and will need to climb up while picking up weapons along the way. They will need to continue to climb to outpace the other player or defeat the other player by eliminating them with a weapon. As players move up and ascend these platforms, the lava from below them starts to rise. Players will need to keep up to pace in order to survive and possibly overtake their opponent.
